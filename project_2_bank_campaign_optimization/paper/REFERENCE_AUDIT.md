## Search scope and exclusions

The update searched peer-reviewed work on direct and database marketing, predictive targeting, decision and policy alignment, temporal validity, probability calibration, leakage, and response versus incremental targeting. Six sources were added: Devriendt et al. (2021), Hitsch et al. (2024), Kleinberg et al. (2015), Shmueli (2010), Smith et al. (2023), and Wedel and Kannan (2016). The literature establishes prior response prediction on these old, widely reused data. The manuscript therefore claims a narrower contribution about the decision to which a performance estimate applies, supported by paired uncertainty across complete information-set specifications.
